How margin is calculated

Profitability % = (Client budget − Expected CTC) ÷ Client budget × 100
  • Healthy – at or above the {{ TARGET_MARGIN|pct }} target margin.
  • Below target – positive but under target. Deficit – expected CTC exceeds the budget.
  • Margin colours – red under {{ MARGIN_RED_AT }}%, green at {{ MARGIN_GREEN_AT }}% and above, and every shade in between as the value moves, so two different margins never share a colour.
  • Leverage index – average of the six radar axes (shown as x / 10). Higher means more bargaining power for the candidate.
  • Action Required – open candidates with a negative margin. The bell and watchlist also include high client urgency combined with high risk of losing the candidate.
